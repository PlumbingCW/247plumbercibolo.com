What to tell the dispatcher

A short, clear description gets the right plumber and the right equipment to your door. Start with your street address and the nearest cross street. Then say what you see: water on a floor, a drain that will not clear, no hot water or a smell.

Say whether water is still running and whether you have found the main shutoff. If you have not, the dispatcher can talk you through finding it. Mention anything that raises the urgency, such as water near outlets, sewage coming up in a tub or a spreading ceiling stain.

It also helps to say what you have already tried. If you used chemical drain cleaner, say so, because the plumber should wear protection. If the water heater is gas and you smell gas, leave the building first, then call 911 and your gas utility from outside.
