Where we send plumbers

Our base is 513 Cibolo Valley Dr in Cibolo, TX, in Guadalupe County. From there we dispatch to the places below, and each town has its own page with local details.

Cibolo itself uses ZIP 78108. Schertz and Selma share ZIP 78154. Universal City uses 78148 and the Randolph Air Force Base ZIP 78150. Converse uses 78109.

What changes from town to town

How coverage works on a call

Coverage is decided by street address, not by ZIP code alone. When you call, the dispatcher asks for the street and the nearest cross street, confirms the town, and then sends a licensed plumber to that address. If you live near the line between two towns, the address settles it in a few seconds.

We dispatch around the clock to all five towns. The line is the same at 2 a.m. as at noon, and same-day appointments are offered when available. We do not promise an arrival time, because the plumber's distance, the number of calls and the job itself all change it.

Planned work follows the same path. If you want a camera look at a sewer line before buying a home in Schertz, or a water heater swap in Converse, call during the day and the dispatcher will arrange a time. The service pages explain what to prepare for each job.

Which utility serves my address

Two things decide who you call when the problem is outside your house: the town and the water provider. In Cibolo, the City of Cibolo and Green Valley Special Utility District both serve customers, and your bill names yours. According to the City, sewer billing moved to GVSUD in November 2025, so a sewer charge may appear on a GVSUD bill.

Which utility serves you depends on your address, so check your bill. Converse runs its own wells alongside Canyon Regional Water Authority supply, and Schertz and Selma each cross county lines. The practical rule is the same everywhere: read the name on the bill, call that utility about anything on the street side of the meter and call a plumber for anything inside the property.
