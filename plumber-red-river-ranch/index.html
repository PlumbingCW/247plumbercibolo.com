Owner, renter or manager, who calls first?

Start with who is responsible for the pipe. An owner of a house decides and pays for repairs. A renter usually reports the problem to the landlord or property manager, and the lease says who approves outside work. We cannot read your lease, so check it before you call a plumber on your own.

Active water is the exception. Close the valve under the fixture or the main for your unit, and if flooding is under way, call the emergency line at once. The plumber who comes out will want to know who approves the work and the price.

What the City and the news say about Red River Ranch

The City of Cibolo's construction page lists "Red River Ranch" Units 1 to 6, as of April 2023. A City redistricting page also refers to "the new Red River Subdivision". Community Impact reported in July 2022 that development began in 2015, with six residential phases, 380 single-family lots, a 324-unit multifamily development and four commercial lots fronting FM 78. D.R. Horton was named as the developer, and the homes had three to five bedrooms.

We do not know which lots are built, how old any house is, or who owns a given building. Niemietz Park at 618 FM 78 is a City place on the same road, not part of the community.

Three kinds of building, three kinds of problem

A house with three to five bedrooms often has more than one bathroom, so more fixtures share one supply. A slow toilet leak in a spare bathroom can run a long time unnoticed. Reading the meter with every tap off settles it, and leak detection pinpoints the spot if the meter keeps moving.

In an apartment building, a drain often serves several units through one shared line. If a ground floor unit backs up while upstairs neighbors drain normally, the blockage may be in the building line, which is the manager's to approve. A cable or clearing the drain visit can clear it.

Commercial lots on FM 78 bring a third set of needs, such as grease from kitchens, public restrooms and larger water heaters. See business plumbing for how that work differs.
