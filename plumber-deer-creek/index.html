How old is your water heater?

Tank water heaters often last roughly 8 to 12 years. That is general trade knowledge, not a Deer Creek statistic. The data plate on the side of the tank carries a serial number that usually encodes the manufacture date, though each brand has its own format.

If the heater came with an older house, it may be near or past that range. If it was replaced, the newer unit's date is the one to use. A leak at the base is the usual end point, because corrosion inside the tank cannot be patched. Planning a water heater replacement before that point is calmer than doing it with water on the floor.
