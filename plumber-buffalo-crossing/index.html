A planned community built in phases

The City of Cibolo's homeowner association listing names both Buffalo Crossing and Reserve at Buffalo Crossing. Its list of subdivisions under construction, as of the April 2023 update, includes Buffalo Crossing II, Units 1 to 5.

According to a July 2022 Community Impact report, Buffalo Crossing II is a 162-acre planned unit development with plans for 480 units on a mix of 60-foot and 70-foot lots. Bella Vista, Gehan Homes and Bellaire Homes were named as builders over about five years. The report also mentions trails linking Schlather Park to the Gatewood subdivision.

Why does that matter at a kitchen sink? Several builders over several years can mean different fixtures, different water heater models and different shutoff locations from one street to the next. Your neighbor's setup may not match yours. The sections described are newer construction, and your closing papers show the exact year.

Dispatch for Buffalo Crossing runs through the same 24/7 line as the rest of Cibolo plumbing, and same-day appointments are offered when available.

Find your shutoffs before something leaks

In many houses the main valve sits near where the supply enters the home or at the meter box, but the spot varies by builder and floor plan. Walk around once and look. Then find the small stop valves under sinks and behind toilets, and the cold supply valve at the water heater.

Each of those valves isolates one problem so the rest of the house keeps water. A failed supply line under a bathroom sink is a good example. Closing its stop valve turns a flooded cabinet into a small repair, such as a new connector or a faucet fix.

Check the builder warranty before you pay anyone

If your home is new enough that a builder warranty may still apply, read it first. Terms differ by builder, and work by someone outside the warranty can affect coverage under some policies. We cannot tell you what yours says.

A leaking drain valve on a water heater is a typical case. A water heater repair fixes a valve, but a leaking tank is a different matter, and the paperwork tells you who handles which.

Whoever you hire, the Texas State Board of Plumbing Examiners has an online license search. Ask for the Responsible Master Plumber, whose license number begins with M.

The City of Cibolo enforces the 2021 International Plumbing Code (IPC). Permit rules vary, so ask the City of Cibolo building department before adding a new sink or laundry hookup.

Which utility sends your water bill

Two providers serve Cibolo, the City of Cibolo and Green Valley Special Utility District (GVSUD), depending on the address. We do not know which one serves each Buffalo Crossing section, so read your bill. Since November 2025 the City no longer bills sewer and GVSUD adds the sewer charge.

That bill tells you who to call for a problem on the street side of the meter. City customers can watch for the leak drop icon in the My Water Advisor portal.
